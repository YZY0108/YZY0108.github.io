CASE 01 2026

TRAJECTORY FORECASTING

NFL Big Data Bowl 2026

TOP 4.8%Silver medal

Can a learned trajectory model improve when the obvious geometry is made explicit?

Approach

Start with role-aware geometric endpoints—receiver attraction, defender mirroring, and inertial motion—then learn the residual with a spatial-temporal Transformer.

Validation

10-fold GroupKFold by game, variable-length masks, Temporal Huber Loss, and a 10-model ensemble to reduce leakage and variance.

  • PyTorch
  • ST-Transformer
  • 167-dim features
  • GroupKFold
